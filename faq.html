👑 Code of Conduct & Guidelines

As a premier platform serving noble Kshatriya lineages, we maintain strict code of conducts to safeguard the community's heritage and respect.

Contact details (phones, email, and social linkages) are encrypted and are only visible to upgraded plan members or accepted matches.
